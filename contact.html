Frequently Asked Questions

What are the admission procedures at Vrindavan Gurukul?

Admission to Vrindavan Gurukul involves the following steps: 1) Fill out the enquiry form or visit the center, 2) Attend a counseling session with our academic advisor, 3) Take a diagnostic test (for certain courses), 4) Complete the registration process with required documents, and 5) Pay the fees to confirm admission.

What documents are required for admission?

You'll need to submit: 1) Recent passport-size photographs (2 copies), 2) Copy of previous year's report card, 3) Aadhaar card copy of student and parent, 4) Address proof, and 5) Transfer certificate (if applicable). For competitive exam courses, additional documents may be required.

What are the batch timings?

We offer multiple batches throughout the day to accommodate different school timings. Morning batches run from 6:30 AM to 8:30 AM, afternoon batches from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM, and evening batches from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Weekend batches are also available for certain courses.

Do you provide transportation facilities?

Currently, we don't provide transportation facilities. However, our center is easily accessible by public transport and we have safe parking spaces for bicycles and two-wheelers. Many students carpool with classmates from nearby areas.

How can parents track their child's progress?

We maintain regular communication with parents through: 1) Monthly parent-teacher meetings, 2) Digital report cards after each test, 3) A parent portal to track attendance and performance, and 4) WhatsApp updates for important announcements. Parents can also schedule meetings with faculty as needed.
